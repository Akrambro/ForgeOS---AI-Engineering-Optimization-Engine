## MathWorks Simulink Design Optimization
Important competitive boundary:
- parameter estimation
- sensitivity analysis
- response optimization
- DOE/Monte Carlo
- controller/plant optimization

ForgeOS differentiation should therefore be based on evaluation orchestration, lineage, uncertainty-aware experiment selection and truth-model verification rather than claiming that generic optimization itself is novel.
